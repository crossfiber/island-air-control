Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your HVAC service in the Florida Keys.

What areas do you serve in the Florida Keys?
We serve the entire Florida Keys chain, with our headquarters in Islamorada. Our primary service area includes Key Largo, Tavernier, Islamorada, Marathon, Big Pine Key, and Key West. We handle both residential and commercial projects throughout the Keys.
Do you offer 24/7 emergency AC repair?
Yes. We are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including nights, weekends, and holidays. When your AC goes down in the Florida Keys heat, call us at (305) 852-4042 and we will respond as quickly as possible.
What brands do you service and install?
We are a certified Mitsubishi Diamond Contractor and authorized Trane dealer. We also install and service American Standard, Rheem, Ruud, Bryant, and Fujitsu. Our technicians perform repairs and maintenance on all makes and ages of heating and air conditioning equipment.
Do you offer financing for new AC installations?
Yes, we offer financing options for qualifying HVAC installations and replacements. We also provide free estimates on all new installations, so you can understand the full scope and cost of your project before committing. Call us at (305) 852-4042 to learn more.
Are you licensed, insured, and bonded?
Absolutely. Island Air Control is fully licensed with the State of Florida (License #CAC1816937), insured, and bonded. We also hold an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau and have been recognized as a Nextdoor Neighborhood Favorite multiple years running.
Do you work on commercial properties?
Yes. Commercial HVAC is one of our specialties. We work with schools, hospitals, airports, museums, shopping centers, resorts, and medical offices. Our commercial services include rooftop and ground unit installation, 24/7 emergency repairs, and preventative maintenance agreements.
What is a Preventative Maintenance Agreement?
Our Preventative Maintenance Agreement includes regularly scheduled inspections and tune-ups of your HVAC system. This keeps your equipment running efficiently, extends its lifespan, prevents costly emergency breakdowns, and helps maintain your manufacturer warranty. We will show you how to monitor, clean, ventilate, and improve the efficiency of your system.
Do you service ice machines and pool heaters?
Yes. We sell, install, and service commercial ice machines from Manitowoc, Ice-O-Matic, and Scotsman. We also handle pool heaters from Rheem, Weatherking, Jandy, and Pentair. If you do not see the equipment or service listed, give us a call. We are happy to help.
